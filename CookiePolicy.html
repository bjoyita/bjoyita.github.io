This Cookie Policy explains how Materials Data Explorer uses cookies and similar technologies on https://bjoyita.github.io/.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on a browser or device. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, tags, software-development tools, and unique identifiers. These technologies can recognize a browser, remember choices, support security, measure usage, and deliver or evaluate advertising.

2. Categories of cookies that may be used

Essential and security technologies

These support basic delivery, security, fraud prevention, network management, and consent preferences. Disabling them may prevent parts of the Website from functioning as intended.

Analytics technologies

Google Analytics or similar tools may use cookies to generate aggregate statistics about visits, referrals, devices, and engagement. These insights help improve the Website.

Advertising technologies

Google AdSense and advertising partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to serve, limit, personalize, and measure advertisements. Personalized advertisements may be based on activity on this Website and elsewhere, subject to consent and applicable law. Even non-personalized advertisements may use cookies for frequency capping, aggregated reporting, fraud prevention, and security.

Social-sharing technologies

ShareThis and social platforms may use cookies or identifiers when sharing buttons or related functions are loaded or activated.

3. Managing your choices

Where required, a consent message may allow visitors to accept, reject, or manage relevant purposes and vendors. Visitors may also:

  • change cookie permissions in their browser;
  • delete previously stored cookies;
  • use private-browsing or tracking-protection features;
  • manage Google ad personalization at Google Ad Settings;
  • use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, where supported; and
  • review the controls offered by other advertising, analytics, and social-media providers.

Blocking cookies may affect advertising, embedded content, sharing features, or other functionality.
